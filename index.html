About Me

Your Guide to Life in Finland

I am a researcher, entrepreneur, and mother of three, living in Finland for nearly a decade. Originally from Pakistan, I have over eight years of administrative experience from my early career before moving to Europe.

In Europe, I completed a master's degree and developed experience as a researcher and entrepreneur, and have also worked in Finnish companies.

Over the years, I have built a life in Finland as an immigrant—raising a family, adapting to the culture, and learning how Finnish systems and society work in everyday life.

Through both my professional background and personal journey, I provide honest, practical guidance to individuals and families who want to understand life in Finland, avoid common mistakes, and make informed decisions.

My consultations are based on both professional experience and lived reality in Finland, supporting both individuals and families.
